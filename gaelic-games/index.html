Kilkenny · Hurling

The Kilkenny Experience

Welcome to Kilkenny, the hurling capital of the world. Home of the legendary Kilkenny Cats - Ireland's most successful hurling team and the pride of the county.

In Kilkenny, hurling is more than just a game. Local clubs form the backbone of social life, where children grow up playing and families gather to support their county or club. With our Kilkenny Experience, you'll learn, play and feel the thrill of the fastest field sport in the world.

The Tradition of Gaelic Football and Hurling

Gaelic Football and Hurling are at the core of Irish culture - ancient sports that have shaped the country's identity for thousands of years. Played in communities nationwide, they bring people together through shared passion, pride, and tradition.

For many Irish people, these games are not just sports - they are a powerful expression of who they are. With Beyond the Pitch, you'll experience Gaelic Games the way locals do: up close, passionate, and deeply connected to community life.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a unique thing to do in Ireland? +

One of the most unique experiences in Ireland is learning to play hurling with a local expert in Kilkenny - the hurling capital of the world - and attending a live match the same day. Unlike typical tourist activities, this connects you directly with Irish communities and a 3,000-year-old tradition.

What is there to do in Ireland besides Dublin? +

Kilkenny is one of Ireland's most rewarding day trips from Dublin - just 90 minutes away. Beyond the Pitch offers a full hurling experience in Kilkenny: a cultural presentation in a traditional Irish pub, a hands-on coaching session on the pitch, and tickets to a live GAA match.

What is hurling and why should I experience it in Ireland? +

Hurling is Ireland's ancient national sport - widely considered the fastest field game in the world. Played with a wooden stick (hurley) and a small ball (sliotar), it has been part of Irish culture for over 3,000 years. Kilkenny is the most successful hurling county in Ireland, making it the ideal place to experience the sport authentically.

Do I need any experience to join the hurling clinic? +

No experience is needed whatsoever. The hurling clinic with local expert James is designed for complete beginners and international visitors. You'll learn the basic skills - ground stroke, hand pass, and solo run - in a relaxed and welcoming environment typical of Irish GAA club culture.

How do I get from Dublin to Kilkenny for this experience? +

Kilkenny is approximately 90 minutes from Dublin by car or bus. Direct coaches run regularly from Dublin's Busáras station. Beyond the Pitch specialises in Dublin day trips to Kilkenny, making this one of the most accessible authentic Irish cultural experiences outside the capital.

What is the best time of year to experience hurling in Ireland? +

The GAA Championship season runs from May to August, which is peak season for live hurling matches. However, hurling clinics and local club matches are available year-round. The All-Ireland Hurling Final takes place every August in Croke Park, Dublin - the pinnacle of the sport.

Is this a good experience for groups or families? +

Yes. The Kilkenny hurling experience works well for families, corporate groups, and solo travellers alike. Groups looking for unique team-building activities in Ireland often find the combination of coaching, culture, and a live match to be a standout experience. Custom group dates can be arranged on request.

How is this different from a standard Ireland tour? +

Most Ireland tours show you the country from a bus window. Beyond the Pitch puts you inside Irish culture - on the pitch with local coaches, in a traditional pub learning the history, and in the stands watching real championship hurling. It is widely considered one of the most authentic and unique cultural experiences available to visitors in Ireland.
