Kilkenny · Hurling

The Kilkenny
Experience

Welcome to Kilkenny, the hurling capital of the world. Home of the legendary Kilkenny Cats - Ireland's most successful hurling team and the pride of the county.

In Kilkenny, hurling is more than just a game. Local clubs form the backbone of social life, where children grow up playing and families gather to support their county or club. With our Kilkenny Experience, you'll learn, play and feel the thrill of the fastest field sport in the world.

What to expect
1

Culture & Heritage

The heart of rural Kilkenny

Your experience begins with a unique introduction to the world of hurling. Meet our expert on the ground: James, a Kilkenny-native with a lifelong love for the sport. Through an engaging presentation, he introduces you to the history, rules and cultural significance of hurling.

This takes place at a location of your choice:

  • —A traditional Irish pub in the heart of the rural village of Freshford, Kilkenny
  • —The intense environment of a players' dressing room, just a stone's throw from Nowlan Park - the iconic stadium of the Kilkenny Cats
2

Step onto the Pitch

Master the skills for yourself

After immersing yourself in the world of hurling, it's time to take the hurley into your own hands. Led by expert local coaches, you'll learn the fundamentals of the game: the ground stroke, hand pass, solo run and hand strike. Soon you'll be ready to take your first shot at the goalposts.

The moment you strike the sliotar for the first time is one you won't forget.

3

Live Hurling Matchday Immersion

Attendance at a live GAA match included

There's only one thing left to complete the experience: watching the very best players in action. As the first whistle sounds, the match explodes into life. Fast, physical and played with incredible skill and precision, hurling is one of the most spectacular sports you'll ever witness.

With a list of local pubs recommended by James, you can follow the footsteps of the fans and experience matchday the true Kilkenny way.

About Kilkenny

Besides being the hurling capital of Ireland, Kilkenny is a vibrant medieval city. Known as the "Marble City," it enchants visitors with cobbled streets, colourful shopfronts, cosy cafés and the impressive Kilkenny Castle overlooking it all.

Dublin · Hurling & Gaelic Football

The Dublin
Experience

In Dublin for a weekend with friends or traveling through Ireland? Add something truly unique to your trip.

With dozens of clubs spread across the city, Gaelic Games are part of everyday life in Dublin. At the centre of it all lies the iconic Croke Park, the symbolic heart of Gaelic Games. With a capacity of over 80.000, it is one of the largest stadiums in Europe. Our Dublin Experience lets you step into the world of Gaelic Games: learn about the sports, give them a go yourself, and experience the electric atmosphere of a live matchday.

Culture & Heritage

Na Fianna GAA Club, Dublin

The experience kicks off at the grounds of Na Fianna, one of Dublin's most vibrant GAA clubs. In a hall-of-fame setting, you'll be introduced to the origins of Gaelic Games and why these sports hold such a central place in Irish culture.

2

Step Onto the Pitch

Gaelic Football, Hurling & Handball

The stage is set, and now it's your turn to step onto the pitch. Under the guidance of experienced coaches, you'll learn the key skills of Gaelic Football and Hurling and get a feel for the speed and intensity of the games. It's the perfect preparation for watching the very best athletes in action later on. You'll also be introduced to a third traditional sport: Gaelic handball.

3

Live Gaelic Games Matchday

Croke park or Parnell park

You'll attend a Gaelic Games match of your choice. From the moment Amhrán na bhFiann plays, you're hooked. We'll prepare you for the full matchday experience, so you can follow in the footsteps of local supporters as they gather in pubs and streets on their way to the stadium.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know

What is a unique thing to do in Ireland? +

One of the most unique things to do in Ireland is joining a small-group hurling or Gaelic Games experience in Kilkenny or Dublin. You learn the sport's history from a local expert, try it yourself on the pitch, and watch a live GAA match alongside real fans. Beyond the Pitch runs these experiences from €129 per person - no prior knowledge required.

Is a live match ticket included in the Gaelic Games experience? +

Yes — and that's what sets Beyond the Pitch apart. Every experience includes a live GAA match ticket. Most Gaelic Games experiences teach you the sport. Ours takes you to an actual match the same day - from the dressing room to the stands. Match access is confirmed personally within 24–48 hours of your booking.

Is the stadium the same size as Croke Park? +

Not always. The club championship fixtures we currently offer are played at Parnell Park, a smaller ground with a more intimate atmosphere - closer to the pitch and closer to the local supporters. County and provincial fixtures, including All-Ireland matches, take place at the much larger Croke Park (over 80,000 capacity). Both offer a genuine GAA matchday experience, just at a different scale - check the fixture details for the exact venue before booking.

Can I watch a live GAA match as part of the experience? +

Yes. Beyond the Pitch curates access to live GAA matches as part of every experience - championship fixtures at Croke Park in Dublin and Nowlan Park in Kilkenny. With the All Ireland season in full swing, there's no better time to be in Ireland for Gaelic Games. Send us your date and we confirm what's available within 24–48 hours. From €129 p.p.

What is hurling and why should I experience it in Ireland? +

Hurling is Ireland's ancient national sport - widely considered the fastest field game in the world. Played with a wooden stick (hurley) and a small ball (sliotar), it has been part of Irish culture for over 3,000 years. Kilkenny is the most successful hurling county in Ireland.

How does the booking process work? +

When you submit a booking request, it is not an instant confirmation - we personally review each request within 24–48 hours. First, send us your details, preferred fixture and group size. We then check host availability and match access to find the best fit for your chosen date. Once reviewed, you'll receive an email confirming what has been arranged and the next steps to finalise your booking. Please note that match access and specific local arrangements remain subject to availability at the time of confirmation.

Can I join if I'm travelling solo? +

Yes. We run open-group experiences where solo travellers join a small group. Message us and we'll match you to the right date.

How long does the full experience take? +

Plan for roughly 5–6 hours from start to final whistle - including the intro session, training, and the match itself.

What happens if it rains? +

Hurling and Gaelic Football are played in all weather - and so is the matchday experience. We'll make sure you're prepared.

Is it possible to visit the All Ireland Final? +

It's a special day, and we understand why it's tempting to ask. In practice though, All-Ireland Final tickets aren't something we can access - they're allocated almost entirely through county and club channels to the supporters who've followed their team all season. We think that's exactly as it should be. What we do offer is access to championship fixtures throughout the year, so you still get the full matchday atmosphere - the crowd, the build-up, the roar - just not the final itself.

Do I need any experience to join the hurling experience +

No experience needed. The hurling experience is designed for complete beginners and international visitors. You'll learn the basic skills in a relaxed and welcoming environment typical of Irish GAA club culture.

How do I get from Dublin to Kilkenny for this experience? +

Kilkenny is approximately 90 minutes from Dublin by car, train or bus. Direct coaches run regularly from Dublin's Busáras station. This is one of the most accessible authentic Irish cultural experiences outside the capital.

What is the best time of year to experience hurling in Ireland? +

Hurling experiences and matches are available year-round.

Can I watch a hurling match in Kilkenny as a visitor? +

Yes. The Kilkenny Experience from Beyond the Pitch includes facilitated access to a live hurling match at UPMC Nowlan Park in Kilkenny. Match access is confirmed personally within 24–48 hours of your booking request. During the Leinster Championship season (April–June), matches are available most weekends. Outside the championship, club fixtures run year-round.

How is this different from a standard Ireland experience? +

Most Ireland experiences show you the country from a bus window. Beyond the Pitch puts you inside Irish culture - on the pitch with local coaches, in a traditional pub learning the history, and in the stands at a live hurling match where possible.
