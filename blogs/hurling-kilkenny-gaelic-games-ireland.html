G'wan the Cats!
Our First Trip to Ireland

🏉 Gaelic Games 📍 Dublin & Kilkenny 📅 February 2026

February was an absolute milestone for Beyond the Pitch - and that's putting it mildly. Alongside making our first major strategic choices as a young company, we made our very first business trip. Destination: Ireland. 🍀

What began as a work visit quickly became something far greater than that. After months of research, YouTube deep-dives and stories from our partner James, the moment had finally arrived - we were going to witness the Gaelic Games with our own eyes.

82,000 Croke Park capacity
90min Dublin to Kilkenny
1 Unforgettable trip

🏟️ Croke Park: The Cathedral

In the legendary Croke Park — an iconic stadium with room for 82,000 spectators and home of the All-Ireland Finals - we stood face to face with Gaelic Football and Hurling for the first time.

The speed. The relentless physicality. Points and goals struck from the most impossible angles. Supporters from rival counties sitting side by side, united in pride for their county, their Ireland.

"It was everything and more than we expected. Sport as a gateway into the soul of a place."

What a spectacle. It was everything and more than we had expected - the kind of experience that reminds you exactly why Beyond the Pitch exists.

The roar of the crowd - nothing quite like it.

Day Two

🐱 Kilkenny: Home of the Cats

The next day might have been even more special. From Dublin we drove to Kilkenny - about 90 minutes south. A city famous for its medieval architecture and, above all, the undisputed hurling capital of Ireland.

We were there for a meeting with our hurling hero: James. Born and raised in Kilkenny, his passion for the sport is completely infectious. He took us to Nowlan Park - home of the Kilkenny Cats - and made sure we experienced the match the right way: surrounded by locals on a packed stand.

⚡ Matchday moment

The man in front of us made one thing crystal clear: TJ Reid was quite possibly the greatest hurler he'd ever seen play. We weren't about to argue.

G'wan! (Irish for: Go on!) rang out from every corner of the ground. The match was a spectacle right until the final minute - Kilkenny earned a free, Nowlan Park held its breath, and when the sliotar sailed between the posts the stadium erupted. A vital win for the Cats. A memory we won't forget.

After the final whistle, James guided us through the stream of supporters, pointing out local spots to eat, before dropping us at the village pub - already full of fans reliving every moment. Exactly how it should be.

Beyond the Stand

We Picked Up a Hurley

Alongside watching the sport, we tried it ourselves - hurley in hand, sliotar on the ground. Let's be honest: it looks easier than it is. Serious respect to everyone who plays this game at a high level.

James is now a permanent partner in our offering. Beyond attending a live match, he runs a session where visitors try the sport themselves and genuinely learn the history and culture behind it. Not a tourist box to tick - a real connection.

Literally: Beyond the Pitch.

Community

🤝 New in Dublin - Meet People Through Gaelic Games

And as if that weren't enough - back in Dublin, we also launched our community project: "New in Dublin - Meet People Through Gaelic Games." A meaningful first step towards the community we're building: one where sport opens the door to real human connection for people who are new to the city.

February set the bar high. We can't wait to see what comes next.
