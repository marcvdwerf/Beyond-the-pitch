Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our journeys.

What is Beyond the Pitch and what makes it different from a travel agency? +

Beyond the Pitch is a cultural sports experience platform that connects curious fans with authentic local sporting traditions around the world. Unlike standard experience providers, we don't package your experience from behind a desk - we connect you directly with the people and communities who live it. Whether that's learning hurling in Kilkenny with a local GAA coach, witnessing the Naadam Festival on the Mongolian steppe. Every experience is built around genuine community access and human connection - curated by locals, not middlemen.

What unique travel experiences do you currently offer? +

We currently offer two cultural sports experiences. In Ireland, you learn hurling with a local expert in Kilkenny and attend a live GAA match — one of the most authentic cultural experiences available to visitors in Ireland, accessible as a day trip from Dublin from €120. In Mongolia, we bring you inside the Naadam Festival — the country's ancient celebration of wrestling, horse racing, and archery. New destinations are added as we build local partnerships worldwide.

Who are these journeys for? +

Our experiences are designed for curious people who want more than standard tourism - people who believe sport is a gateway to culture and human connection. You don't need to be a sports expert or particularly fit. Whether you're solo, a couple, an expat looking to connect locally, or a group of friends seeking something genuinely different, you'll feel at home at a Beyond the Pitch experience.

What is included in the price of a journey? +

Each journey includes match or event tickets, a passionate local expert guide, curated cultural experiences, and selected meals. Prices start from €120 for the Ireland hurling experience, and €400 for the Mongolia Naadam trip. International flights and accommodation are generally not included, but we always provide recommendations to help you plan your full trip.

Are the groups small? +

Yes, intentionally. We keep groups small - typically between 8 and 14 people - to ensure every experience feels personal rather than packaged, and to avoid overwhelming the local communities we visit. Small groups also mean you personally connect with every person in the group, which is one of the things our participants value most.

Where is Beyond the Pitch based and where do you operate? +

Beyond the Pitch is based in the Netherlands and offers experiences worldwide. Current destinations include Ireland and Mongolia, with new destinations added as we build partnerships with local communities, clubs, and guides. Beyond our experiences, we also produce original sports culture content available for media licensing, and work with brands on sponsorship and co-created storytelling.

Do you also offer media licensing or brand partnerships? +

Yes. Alongside our cultural experiences, Beyond the Pitch produces original video and photo content from some of the world's most compelling sporting traditions. This content is available for licensing to broadcasters, publishers, and digital platforms. We also partner with brands that share our values of culture, authenticity, and community - through sponsored experiences, branded content, and co-created storytelling across TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube. Get in touch via our partners page to explore what's possible.

Beyond the Pitch × Naadam Festival Mongolia

Join us for an unforgettable three-day journey into the heart of Mongolia's most important celebration: the Naadam Festival.

Program

  • July 10: Zuunmod - Experience the local Naadam Festival just outside Ulaanbaatar.
  • July 11: Ulaanbaatar - Be part of the official opening ceremony and watch wrestling and archery at the stadium.
  • July 12: The Steppe - See the famous horse races and meet trainers to learn about nomadic traditions.

About Naadam

Naadam is Mongolia's national holiday, centered on three traditional sports: wrestling, horse racing, and archery. It is a living tribute to the country's nomadic heritage, filled with music, dance, food, and vibrant community spirit.

Why travel with us

Our program gives you access to events that are normally hard for visitors to attend, including the opening ceremony. With guidance from a local expert, you'll experience Naadam not as a tourist, but as part of the celebration.
