What makes Beyond the Pitch different from a travel agency? +

Beyond the Pitch is a cultural sports experience organiser, not a travel agency or tour operator. We don't sell travel packages or arrange flights and accommodation. Our focus is the experience itself - bringing participants closer to the communities, traditions and people behind the sport. Participants arrange their own travel independently.

What is included in the price? +

Each experience includes reserved seating at a live sporting event, guidance from a local expert, and curated cultural activities. Prices start from €120 (Ireland) and $400 (Mongolia). Flights, accommodation and travel arrangements are not included - participants arrange their own independently.

Who are these experiences for? +

Curious people who want more than standard tourism. You don't need to be a sports expert. Whether you're solo, a couple, or a group of friends looking for something genuinely different - you'll feel at home at a Beyond the Pitch experience.

Are the groups small? +

Yes, intentionally. We keep groups to a maximum of 12 participants - so the experience feels personal, not packaged, and local communities are respected throughout.

Do you offer media licensing or brand partnerships? +

Yes. We produce original video and photo content from compelling sporting traditions and offer it for licensing to broadcasters, publishers and digital platforms. We also collaborate with brands on experience sponsorships and co-created storytelling. Get in touch via our partners page.
