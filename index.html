What makes Beyond the Pitch different from a travel agency? +

A travel agency books your transport and hotel. We do something different: we curate access to the sport itself — through guides who have spent their lives inside it. James has played hurling in Kilkenny his whole life. Kamil has followed Czech hockey through everything the country has been through. You're not buying a tour. You're getting access that most visitors never find.

What is included in the price? +

Each experience includes reserved seating at a live sporting event, guidance from a local expert, and curated cultural activities. Flights, accommodation and travel arrangements are not included.

Who are these experiences for? +

Curious people who want more than standard tourism. You don't need to be a sports expert - you'll feel at home at a Beyond the Pitch experience.

Do you offer media licensing or brand partnerships? +

Yes. We produce original video and photo content from compelling sporting traditions and offer it for licensing to broadcasters, publishers and digital platforms. We also collaborate with brands on experience sponsorships and co-created storytelling. Get in touch via our partners page.
