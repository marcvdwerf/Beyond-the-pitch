Your Path to Belonging

1

The Entrance: Join the Group

Jump into our "New in Dublin" WhatsApp community. It’s where everything happens—from last-minute training updates to weekend pub trips and match-day ticket swaps.

2

The Training: Move Your Body

Weekly social training sessions with our partner clubs (Na Fianna, St. Jude's). We don't care if you've never held a hurley; we teach you the basics in a low-pressure, high-fun environment.

3

The Belonging: Real Connection

This is where Beyond the Pitch lives. After training, we head to the clubhouse or a local pub. You’re not just an expat in a city; you’re a member of a club. That is Ireland's secret sauce.
