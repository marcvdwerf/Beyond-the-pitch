The -
Dublin Way.

Authentic integration doesn't happen at a networking event. It happens on the pitch.

Beyond the Pitch is opening the field for anyone calling Dublin home. We don't do formal introductions; we use the grit of Hurling and the soul of Gaelic Football to turn strangers into friends.

Whether you've been here three days or thirty years: when the ball is thrown in, we are one collective.

Frequently Asked Questions

When & Where?

The exact pitch will be chosen by the tribe. First session starts in early May 2026.

What's Included?

Two casual pitch sessions per month, a monthly pub meet‑up, WhatsApp community, access to GAA match trips, and cultural outings to help make Ireland your new home.

Equipment Needed?

None. All equipment is provided. Just bring trainers and weather‑appropriate gear.

Skill Level?

Absolute beginners welcome. We start with touch versions — zero contact, maximum craic.

Work & Play.

Looking to help your international team feel truly at home in Dublin? We organize small-scale, authentic Gaelic sports and cultural experiences that transform colleagues into a local community. Not your standard teambuilding, but a real connection to the Irish soul.
