Meet People in Dublin Through Gaelic Games

Beyond the Pitch is a Dublin community for internationals and locals who want to connect through sport, culture and shared experiences. Join a beginner-friendly Gaelic Games session, a pub social, or a match day - and get to know Dublin through the people who live it.

No experience needed. You can come on your own. We make the first step easy.

How it works

A better way to arrive in Dublin

This is not a networking night and not a traditional sports club. We use Gaelic Games as a natural, local way to help people meet - on the pitch, around the city, and through shared match-day moments.

What to expect

Your first time should feel easy

The biggest friction point is usually not sport - it’s uncertainty. So we make the first experience clear and welcoming.

No experience required

Most first-timers have never played before. Sessions are adapted for beginners.

You can come alone

A lot of people do. This is designed to help newcomers meet people without awkward formality.

Bring trainers and weather-ready clothes

We keep logistics simple. If anything specific is needed, we’ll tell you in advance.

It’s social before it’s serious

The goal is connection first, performance second. That is what makes the format work.

FAQ

Questions people ask before joining

Do I need experience to join?

No. Most people join as complete beginners. Sessions are designed to be social, welcoming and beginner-friendly.

Can I come on my own?

Yes. Many people do. This community is built for newcomers, internationals and locals who want to meet people in a low-pressure way.

Where do sessions happen?

Usually at a confirmed Dublin location such as Na Fianna or another suitable pitch. Exact details are shared before the event.

What happens after I register interest?

You’ll hear from us with the next suitable session, social or match-day update, plus relevant community information.
