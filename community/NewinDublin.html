Integration FAQ

No experience? No problem.

Most of our members have never touched a sliotar or a gaelic football. We focus on fun and basic skills.

Who can join?

Anyone living in Dublin looking for a deeper connection to the city's culture and people.

What equipment do I need?

Just bring your sports shoes. We provide all the hurls, helmets, and footballs you need.
