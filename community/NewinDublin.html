Expat FAQ

Can I join without experience?

Yes! 90% of our members have never held a hurl before joining. We specialize in teaching the basics in a social, low-pressure environment.

Is this a real GAA club?

We partner with official clubs. We provide the social "on-ramp" for people who aren't ready for competitive leagues but want the community feel.

What do I need to bring?

Just sports gear and a willing spirit. We provide the equipment (hurls, helmets, balls) for all training sessions.
