Culture
In Motion.

Authentic integration doesn't happen at a networking event. It happens on the pitch, in the rain, while fighting for the same ball. That is the Dublin way.

Beyond the Pitch brings together those who don't just want to see Dublin, but want to breathe it. We use Gaelic Games to forge a shared identity. Whether you've been here for three days or thirty years: on the pitch, we are one.

Dublin Integration FAQ

Can I join without experience?

Yes! 90% of our members are beginners. We focus on fun and basic skills in Hurling and Gaelic Football.

Is it just for expats?

No. We are a unified collective. Local Dubliners (Captains) are the heartbeat of the community.
