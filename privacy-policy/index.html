01

Who we are

Beyond the Pitch ("Beyond the Pitch", "we", "us") is an international online platform that brings together sport, culture, and travel. We act solely as a third-party intermediary and marketplace connecting travelers with independent local providers of sports and cultural experiences via www.travelbeyondthepitch.com and associated services (the "Platform").

We are not a tour operator. We do not execute, organize, or carry out travel trips ourselves. All experiences are provided directly by third-party local partners. Beyond the Pitch is not responsible for the execution, quality, safety, or outcomes of any trips or services booked through the Platform.

We are the data controller for personal data processed through the Platform under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Dutch Implementation Act (Uitvoeringswet AVG).

02

What data we collect

We collect personal data only to facilitate connections between you and local providers.

Account details

Name, email, password, phone, country, language, optional profile photo.

Booking facilitation

Traveler names, contacts, passport/ID (if required by provider), dates, dietary needs, emergency contacts.

Payment facilitation

Billing details. Full card data is handled exclusively by third-party processors. We store no card numbers or CVV codes.

Communications

Inquiries, feedback, newsletters.

User content

Reviews and photos you share.

Technical data

IP address, device info, usage patterns, cookies.

Partner data

Local providers share minimal info needed for bookings (e.g., attendance confirmation).

03

Why we use your data

We process data strictly to run the Platform as an intermediary. Below is an overview of purposes and their legal bases.

Contract
Facilitate bookings by passing your details to selected local providers; manage accounts and customer support.
Legitimate interests
Analyze Platform usage, prevent fraud, and improve matching between travelers and providers.
Consent
Marketing emails and offers (opt out at any time); non-essential cookies.
Legal obligation
Tax and accounting records; requests from competent authorities.
Vital interests
Emergency data sharing if required for your safety (via providers).

You may withdraw your consent at any time without affecting processing that has already taken place.

04

Cookies and tracking

Essential cookies are required for the Platform to function. Other cookies – such as Google Analytics and Meta Pixel – help us improve matching and advertising.

A cookie banner on your first visit lets you accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can also update your preferences at any time via your browser settings.

05

Who receives your data

We share only what is strictly necessary to connect you with providers.

Local providers
Name, contact details, special needs, and emergency contact information – solely for the execution of the booking. They are independent data controllers; please review their privacy policies.
Processors
Payment companies (e.g., Stripe), hosting and email tools, analytics – bound by contracts to process data only on our instructions.
Authorities / legal
Where required by law or to protect rights and safety.
Aggregated data
Anonymous statistics for Platform analysis (no individual identification possible).
Liability note: Local providers are solely responsible for the execution of trips. Beyond the Pitch does not endorse or guarantee their services.
06

International transfers

Because users and providers are based worldwide, data may be transferred outside the European Economic Area (EEA). We safeguard your data through EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and European Commission adequacy decisions.

07

Data retention

We retain personal data no longer than necessary.

Accounts & bookings

Active period + 7 years after last activity (tax/legal requirements).

Marketing

Until you opt out.

Support

3 years after last contact.

After the retention period expires, data is deleted or anonymized.

10

Security & changes

We protect your personal data with appropriate technical and organisational measures, including encryption and access controls. In the event of a data breach that poses a risk to you, we will notify the competent authorities within 72 hours.

Any changes to this Privacy Policy will be published on this page. You will be notified of material changes.
