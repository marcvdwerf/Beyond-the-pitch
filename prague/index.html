The route

Eight stops.
One country's story.

From 1918 independence to the Nagano gold -moments, streets and symbols that help Czech hockey make sense.

01 · 1918 · National Theatre

A nation is born

Why hockey became part of how Czechoslovakia saw itself and what happened when that symbol was threatened.

02 · 1938 · Žofín Island

The first rink

Prague's original hockey stadium, on the brink of a Europe sliding into war.

03 · 1950 · Zlatá Hospůdka

The state turns on its own

National hockey players arrested by the regime, the emotional centre of the walk.

04 · 1950s · Myslíkova Street

Sport as performance

How bodies, crowds and rituals became propaganda and how ordinary people moved within that.

05 · Interlude · Lucerna

A Prague what-if

A lighter, local detail, the cultural palace that was once almost a hockey venue.

06 · 1968–69 · Wenceslas Sq.

Tanks, then goals

How victories over the USSR became political anger, hope and collective feeling on the ice.

07 · 1989 · Political Prisoners St.

Velvet Revolution

From fear and control to self-definition and the later split into two nations, two hockey stories.

08 · 1998–2001 · Hall of Fame

Nagano & memory

The route ends where pride becomes tangible players, trophies and the moments that still live.

Good to know

Common questions.

Do I need to know anything about ice hockey?+

Nothing at all. Most guests have never watched a hockey match. The history makes the sport make sense -not the other way around.

When can I do the live match version?+

Czech Extraliga runs September – April. Outside the season, Version A (walking + Hall of Fame) is a complete experience on its own.

What if no match is available for my date?+

We'll tell you clearly within 24–48 hours, suggest the closest alternative, or offer Version A. If nothing fits, there's no charge.

Is the history heavy? OK for all ages?+

Real and honest, not sanitised, but never shock-led. Teens and adults find it compelling. Younger children welcome with some parental context.

I'm travelling solo - can I still join?+

Yes. We regularly pair solo guests with other small groups on the same date. Just mention it in the form.

How is this different from a regular Prague walking tour?+

Most tours show you the same five landmarks and call it history. This one uses sport as the thread -and sport turns out to be one of the most honest ways to read a culture.
